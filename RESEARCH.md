## Aircraft nomenclature

The correct requested name is **Falcon 900EX EASy**, not “Falcon 900 X Easy”. Dassault's own historical timeline uses the former name:
https://timeline.dassault-aviation.com/en/shaping-the-future/

The current manufacturer page for the distinct Falcon 900LX was inspected, including its actual aircraft imagery. This was used only to understand the general tri-engine family. No 900LX specifications have been reused as 900EX EASy specifications.
https://www.dassault-aviation.com/en/civil/falcon-family/falcon-900lx/
